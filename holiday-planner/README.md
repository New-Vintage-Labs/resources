# Holiday Campaign Planner

A one-page October–December planner that shows how your holiday calendar compares with your revenue goal.

## Build your own

1. Download [SPEC_0.5.md](SPEC_0.5.md).
2. Open a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT, upload it, and say **"Build my holiday planner."**
3. Explore the fictional example or answer the setup questions with your own numbers and calendar.
4. Customize the plan on the page or in that same chat.

## Make the numbers live

The free planner uses numbers you enter. To connect your existing planner:

1. [Start a New Vintage Pro trial or activate Pro](https://www.newvintage.ai/pricing). Install New Vintage through Commerce7, finish account setup, and connect your data sources. The Claude connector requires an active Pro entitlement and synced data.
2. [Connect New Vintage to Claude](https://www.newvintage.ai/mcp). In Claude, go to **Settings → Connectors**, choose **New Vintage**, sign in, allow access, and enable it for the chat where you built your planner.
3. Upload [UPGRADE_0.5.md](UPGRADE_0.5.md) in that same chat and say **"Connect my planner to New Vintage."** Keep the same planner and artifact.

## Add VIP gifters

After the upgrade, upload [VIP-GIFTERS_0.1.md](VIP-GIFTERS_0.1.md) in the same chat and say **"Add VIP gifters to my planner."** It adds a tab for your biggest gift-givers, their order history and whether they have ordered this season.

The VIP add-on includes draft queries. Have Claude check the listed tables and columns against your connected winery before using its figures.

## Start directly with New Vintage

If you already have Pro access and synced data, enable the connector and upload [SPEC_0.5_NVPRO.md](SPEC_0.5_NVPRO.md) to a new Claude chat. Say **"Build my holiday planner."** This replaces the initial build and upgrade steps; add VIP gifters afterward in the same chat.
