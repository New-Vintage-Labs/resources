# Holiday Campaign Planner

A one-page October–December planner that shows whether your holiday calendar reaches your revenue goal.

## Build your own in 5 minutes

1. Download [SPEC_0.4.md](SPEC_0.4.md).
2. Open a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT and upload it.
3. Say "Build my holiday planner," and have your calendar handy.
4. Answer a few questions.

## Make the numbers live

When you're ready, turn on the New Vintage connector in Claude, upload [UPGRADE_0.4.md](UPGRADE_0.4.md) in the same chat, and say "Connect my planner to New Vintage."

## What's in this folder

- [SPEC_0.4.md](SPEC_0.4.md): the instructions you upload to build your planner.
- [UPGRADE_0.4.md](UPGRADE_0.4.md): the add-on that connects your planner to New Vintage so the numbers stay current.
- [reference/holiday-planner.html](reference/holiday-planner.html): an example of the finished page, for reference.

## Updates

The file names carry version numbers, and this folder always has the latest.
