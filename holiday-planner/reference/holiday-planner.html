How to use this planner

A two-minute tour. Everything here saves as you go.

1. What this page answers

Does your October–December plan reach your revenue goal, and how is the season tracking? The verdict at the top says it in one sentence, and it updates as you change anything.

2. Reading the verdict and goal bar

The bar shows planned revenue split by program. The solid tick is your goal, the dashed tick is last year, and the triangle is actual revenue so far. The program list beside it shows planned and actual revenue for each program. Click one to filter the calendar.

3. Adding and editing activities

Click an empty spot in a lane to add an activity right there, in that lane and week. Click any chip to open it. Change its lane or start week with the dropdowns. List size, conversion and average order make its projected revenue.

4. Bringing in your own calendar (import)

  1. Choose More, then Import activities, then Download the template.
  2. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets. Put one activity on each row: its title, lane, start week and so on. Keep the column headings.
  3. Save or download it as CSV.
  4. Back here, choose Import activities, pick the file, then choose Add to my plan or Replace my plan.
  5. Check the preview. It lists any rows that need fixing. Then press Import.

Your goal, key dates and last year stay as they are.

5. Typed vs Calculated, and your weekly habit

You type the plan, the goal and actuals. Anything marked Calculated comes from those. Each week:

  • update actual revenue and the booked-through week
  • add actuals to activities that have finished
  • check pacing

6. Make it yours by asking

You can ask the assistant in the chat where you built this page. For example:

  • Add a Black Friday SMS to SMS subscribers in the week of Nov 23.
  • Change the club shipment list size to 2,300.
  • Add a wholesale program.

It updates the page for you. If you've edited here since, download the CSV and attach it to your request, so it starts from your latest plan.

7. Making the numbers live

When you're ready, turn on the New Vintage connector in Claude and say Connect my planner to New Vintage in this chat. Actual revenue, last year and each email and SMS result then fill in from your systems. You can still type any number yourself. More, then Connect live data, has the steps.

8. Saving and sharing

  • Changes save by themselves.
  • Download CSV opens in Excel or Google Sheets.
  • Print or save as PDF for sharing.

Connect live data

Right now you type every number. With New Vintage connected, actual revenue, last year, lane defaults and each email and SMS result fill in from Commerce7, Klaviyo, Mailchimp and RedChirp, and update when you open this page.

  1. Turn on the New Vintage connector in Claude's settings (a New Vintage Pro account).
  2. In the chat where you built this planner, say Connect my planner to New Vintage. You may be asked to upload one more file, from wherever you got this planner.
  3. Answer a few questions, such as how your winery marks corporate gifting orders. Your activities stay as they are.

Anything synced can still be typed by hand, and events stay typed.
